# 9. Activity of Day 9

## Final Presentation & Course Reflection

I compiled complete project documentation and reflected on the 9-day journey from CO3 nameplate concept to finished walnut product, documenting lessons learned and techniques mastered across digital fabrication methods.

#### Activity 1: Complete Project Documentation Assembly

Assembled final documentation package: CAD files (FreeCAD, STEP, STL, DXF), CAM toolpaths (G-code), technical drawings (PDF), bill of materials, QC checklist, process photos (120+), and time-lapse video. Total project cost: \.42 (walnut \, finishing \, prototyping \.42). Active work: ~15 hours; passive time (printing, drying, curing): ~33 hours.

![Workflow Overview](../images/day_9/Workflow Overview.png)

#### Activity 2: Before/After Transformation Documentation

Photographed CO3 evolution: Day 1 sketch → Day 2 CAD model → Day 5 cardboard template → Day 6 PLA prototype → Day 7 raw CNC walnut → Day 8 finished walnut. Tested lighting angles (overhead, 45° side, backlighting) to showcase letter depth and grain patterns. Finished piece looks professional and exhibition-ready.

#### Activity 3: Lessons Learned & Skill Mastery

**Key Takeaways:** (1) Iterate in cheap materials first (cardboard \.50, PLA \) before expensive final (walnut \), (2) Parametric CAD enables rapid refinement, (3) DFM thinking prevents fabrication disasters, (4) CNC precision (±0.1mm) combined with hand finishing creates museum-quality results, (5) Documentation is part of design—reproducibility matters.

**Skills Acquired:** Parametric solid modeling (FreeCAD), CAM toolpath generation (Fusion 360), laser cutting (CO₂ cutter), 3D printing (FDM), CNC milling (3-axis router), finishing craftsmanship (sanding, staining, sealing), technical documentation standards.
