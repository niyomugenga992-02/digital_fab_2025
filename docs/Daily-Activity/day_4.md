# 4. Activity of Day 4

## Materials & Fabrication Methods

I evaluated four candidate materials (plywood, hardwood, aluminum, acrylic) against design criteria and selected walnut hardwood for its optimal balance of aesthetics, machinability, and durability for the final CO3 nameplate.

#### Activity 1: Material Options Evaluation

I tested and compared: Plywood (cheap, easy to machine), Hardwood (premium appearance, harder to machine), Aluminum (industrial look, requires coolant), Acrylic (modern, risk of melting). Created a weighted decision matrix considering appearance (30%), machinability (25%), durability (20%), cost (15%), learning value (10%).

#### Activity 2: Material Selection Decision

Walnut hardwood scored highest (4.1/5): rich chocolate color, machines cleanly at 18,000 RPM with 800mm/min feed, takes stain beautifully, and creates a portfolio-quality final piece. Selected walnut as final material over plywood (inferior appearance) and aluminum (too difficult for learning).

#### Activity 3: Fabrication Parameters Planning

Determined CNC milling parameters for walnut: spindle speed 18,000-20,000 RPM, feed rate 800mm/min for roughing and 600mm/min for finishing, 1mm stepdown for roughing and 0.25mm for finishing. Ordered 160×100×12mm walnut blank (~\).
