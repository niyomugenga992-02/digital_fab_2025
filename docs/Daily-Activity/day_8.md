# 8. Activity of Day 8

## Finishing & Surface Enhancement: CO3 Nameplate Completion

I transformed the raw CNC-milled walnut into a polished, museum-quality finished nameplate through progressive sanding, dark walnut staining, and multi-coat polyurethane sealing.

#### Activity 1: Progressive Sanding (120 → 220 → 400 grit)

Sanded with 120-grit to remove CNC tool marks (15 min). Wiped dust, sanded with 220-grit to remove 120-grit scratches (15 min). Final sanding with 400-grit using light pressure created silky-smooth surface (10 min). Result: Glass-smooth walnut, ready for stain. Touch-tested: fingernail no longer catches on any surface marks.

#### Activity 2: Dark Walnut Staining

Applied Minwax Dark Walnut oil stain using foam brush (3 min application). Let penetrate for 8 minutes, wiped off excess with cotton rag. Walnut transformed from pale tan to rich chocolate brown; grain patterns now dramatically visible. Let cure 4-6 hours until not tacky.

#### Activity 3: Protective Polyurethane Coating (3 coats)

Applied water-based satin polyurethane coat 1 (2 hrs dry), light-sanded with 320-grit, applied coat 2 (2 hrs dry), sanded, applied coat 3. Final cure: 24 hours. Result: Satin sheen, silky-smooth surface, protected for years. CO3 nameplate now looks like a high-end furniture piece with professional finish.
